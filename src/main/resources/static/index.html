How LendLoop Works

Borrowing and lending between students on campus is streamlined, safe, and transparent.

1

1. List or Discover

Browse campus textbooks, calculators, and lab tools available nearby, or list your idle equipment to support peers in your department.

2

2. Request & Coordinate

Send a borrow request with your preferred dates and contact information. Owners review requests and approve loans in one click.

3

3. Handover & Return

Once approved, phone numbers and emails unlock exclusively for both students to meet on campus, exchange the item, and coordinate return.

Platform Pillars

Built for Real Campus Life

LendLoop makes borrowing and lending practical, economical, and privacy-protected.

Borrow with Ease

Need a specialized engineering calculator for midterms or an expensive reference book for three weeks? Borrow it from a senior classmate.

  • Zero rental fees or platform commissions
  • Flexible date ranges matching exam cycles
  • Transparent item condition details

Lend & Help Peers

Don't let valuable tools collect dust on your shelf. List spare textbooks and equipment so they keep delivering value throughout your campus.

  • Full control over loan durations & approvals
  • Automated return reminders & tracking
  • Real-time dashboard for all your listed items

Privacy-First Contact

Personal phone numbers and personal emails are never exposed on public item listings. Contact info is securely unlocked only after a loan is accepted.

  • Locked state for pending or rejected requests
  • Direct Call, Email, and Copy Number tools
  • Verified college email student logins
